**Competitor lesson:** POS, printing, inventory, offline mode, and even online ordering are not uniquely new. Do not position basic features as market firsts.

## Differentiation hypotheses

1. Reliable single-terminal PWA with a clear path to Tauri hardware support.
2. Transparent configuration and white-label modules without installing different software per restaurant.
3. Shift/cash reconciliation and later actual profit intelligence, with transparent estimated versus confirmed data.
4. Attractive self-service onboarding and predictable subscriptions (target $10–15 equivalent per restaurant/month).
5. Practical local support, supported printer list and retention-friendly cancellation/export.
6. Structured data allowing opt-in AI insights later without making AI a launch dependency.

These are **hypotheses to validate**, not proven gaps in other products. Price cannot be the only moat. As of the planning discussion, the low end of Pakistani competitor pricing is already crowded; our benefit must be operationally measurable.

## Customer research plan

Interview 20–30 Karachi restaurant owners/staff across takeaway, cafés and dine-in; observe at least five peak shifts. Record current vendor/contract, actual feature usage, printer/devices, outage frequency, cash discrepancy incidents, menu edits, training needs and *willingness to pay*. Pilot 3–5 restaurants with parallel operation before exclusive cutover.

## Market constraints

PKR local billing, Pakistani phone formats, recipes/units, Urdu-ready interface, regional taxes (e.g., Sindh revenue requirements), cash/card/transfer/Raast distinctions, Foodpanda integration only through allowed methods and payment/settlement reconciliation. Certification and partnerships remain separate milestones.
